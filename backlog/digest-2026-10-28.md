## How Do Coding-Agent Harnesses Self-Evolve? An Empirical Study of Retrospective Adaptation
- Source: https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.34938
- Type: research paper (Treude, Baltes; Oct 26 2026)
- What: a `/retro` skill reviewed 30 Claude Code sessions on SWE-bench Verified and produced 125 harness-change proposals. 12 adaptations were tested in fresh sessions on later issues from the same repos (5 runs with, 5 without). Of the 7 informative tests, 2 eliminated the targeted action (both covered the exact problem later sessions hit), 1 reduced it (5/5 to 2/5), and 4 left it unchanged. One incomplete known-failures file added work on an unrelated control task (0/5 to 4/5 baseline comparisons). 18 of 19 proposals not based on session events were lint checks the skill recommends by default; a bundled lint line nearly doubled session time (88 s to 156 s median).
- What to do with it: nexus-smash's retro (`skills/next.md` Step 6) writes footguns and tests but never checks whether a change helped later. Record per footgun the trap it targets, then have `/refine` count later runs that hit the trap with and without the change, and drop or narrow ones that show no effect. Prefer repo-wide facts (the test command) over environment snapshots (lists of failing tests); the paper found the latter go stale and can add work.
